BotaniX AI

Flower species classification with deep learning

Upload a flower photo and BotaniX AI names the species out of 102, with a confidence score for its top three guesses. The model runs in your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.

    How it works

    Everything happens inside your browser: no server, no upload.

    Upload a photoAny JPG, PNG or WebP image of a flower.
    PreprocessResize to 256, centre-crop to 224×224, ImageNet normalisation, exactly as in training.
    MobileNetV2 inferenceThe fine-tuned network (ONNX) runs with ONNX Runtime Web.
    Top-3 speciesSoftmax confidence for the three most likely flowers.

    Test performance

    • Accuracy: 97.72% (1,201 of 1,229 correct)
    • Macro F1-score: 0.9725
    • Misclassified: 28 images

    Error analysis

    • Most errors are look-alike species
    • Petunia → tree mallow (2×)
    • Sword lily → canna lily, rose → camellia, garden phlox → petunia, tree poppy → windflower (1× each)

    Dataset & model

    Transfer learning with a small, fast network that is easy to ship to the web.

    Dataset

    • Oxford 102 Flowers (Visual Geometry Group, University of Oxford)
    • 8,189 images, 102 species, 40 to 258 images per class
    • Official splits merged, then re-split 70/15/15 with seed 42
    • Train 5,732 · Validation 1,228 · Test 1,229
    • Training-only augmentation: random crop, flip, rotation, colour jitter

    Model & training

    • MobileNetV2 pretrained on ImageNet, new 102-class head, all layers fine-tuned
    • AdamW, learning rate 3e-4, cosine schedule, batch size 32
    • Cross-entropy loss, 10 epochs, best epoch chosen on validation accuracy
    • PyTorch on Google Colab (T4 GPU), about 8.4 minutes

    Deployment

    • Exported to ONNX (9.4 MB, single file)
    • Static site on Vercel using ONNX Runtime Web
    • Warns when top confidence is below 50%
    • predict.py and evaluate.py in the repository reproduce predictions and test metrics
